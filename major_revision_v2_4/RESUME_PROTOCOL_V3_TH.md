This amendment does not alter the frozen v2 scientific design, cases, agents,
allocation arms, prompts, verifier, or metrics. The first two v2 recovery blocks
were independently audited. During V24_BLOCK_03 the controlling process stopped
without a terminal marker. Two requests have `request_started.json` but no
provider response or summary: CF_FIT `pandas_121:repair` and CF_FIT
`MAIN_ADULT_02:train`. Their provider outcome is unknown, not model failure.
The complete block-03 raw tree and two prior audit capsules are hashed in a
separate v3 manifest before any further paid call. Nothing in block 03 is
retried, repaired, substituted, or used as a paired comparison.

The remaining prespecified blocks 04–12 retain their v2 IDs, order, cases, and
arms. A new supervisor checks the v2 lock, v3 manifest, incident snapshot,
prior audits, and source hashes before each block. It launches each block once,
performs the independent audit, then proceeds. Any started incomplete block or
failed audit stops the queue. A hidden detached WSL process is used to avoid
binding paid work to an interactive chat/session. The credential is inherited
as a process-only environment variable; it must not enter a command line,
file, manifest, or Git commit. Logs and heartbeat are operational metadata.

The final analysis, if all remaining blocks qualify, contains 11 complete
paired blocks (01, 02, 04–12). It explicitly reports one interrupted planned
block, nine started calls in that block, and the two unknown outcomes. The
previously exposed block-01 sensitivity contains 10 complete blocks. Neither
set is represented as a completed 12-block study, a powered population sample,
or evidence of equivalence. Repository-cluster intervals remain descriptive.
The separate statistical extension needs new independent source repositories,
bugs, and public ML datasets under a new frozen protocol.
