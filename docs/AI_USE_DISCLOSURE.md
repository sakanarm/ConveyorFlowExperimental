# AI-use and annotation disclosure

## Task-difficulty labels

Task difficulty was operationalized with three isolated, role-conditioned passes of the same underlying LLM and a separate adjudication procedure. The passes are not independent human experts, and their agreement is evidence of procedural stability rather than human inter-rater agreement or external ground truth. Prompts, blinded packets, raw role outputs, agreement statistics, adjudication records, and frozen labels are retained in `expert_labels/`.

Human-expert validation is pending and must be completed or the claims must remain explicitly limited to machine-generated operational labels before journal submission.

## Research assistance

Generative AI was used as a research assistant for drafting, code review, document structuring, and visual preparation. The author reviewed the outputs, selected the final methods, executed the experiments, checked the results, and remains responsible for the manuscript, code, statistical interpretation, citations, and disclosures. AI is not an author.

The final acknowledgement/disclosure must be adapted to the policy of the selected journal. No AI detector can reliably establish authorship of prose; the appropriate controls are transparent disclosure, retained provenance, claim-to-evidence traceability, citation verification, and human editorial review.
